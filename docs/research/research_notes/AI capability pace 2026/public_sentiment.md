## A. Public polling on AI benefits vs risks (2026)

### Takeaway
Concern is high and rising in most series, with the steepest rises among young adults. **But most of the measured shift predates the incident.** Pew's headline number has been flat at about 50–52% since 2023. Gallup's and the UK ONS's rises were fielded in May–June 2026. Quinnipiac's "more harm than good" dipped slightly after the incident (55% in Mar to 53% in Sep). The one clear post-incident jump is Fox News's favourability gap (from -9 in July to -23 in September). Post-incident polls show very large majorities for slowing down and for safety standards, but those are "pace" questions, not "benefit vs risk" trend lines.

## B. AI in the November 2026 US midterms (election day 3 Nov)

### Takeaway
AI was salient, but mostly **through data centres**: electricity bills, water and local land use. AI safety or loss of control was not the frame. Most voters called AI and data centres a major factor, yet few called it *the* top issue. Anti-data-centre candidates and ballot measures did very well, within a broader Democratic wave. AI-industry super PAC money mostly failed in contested races. The best-known pro-safety candidate, Alex Bores, lost his June primary despite heavy safety-PAC support.

- **[FOR][secondary]** NBC/national exit poll (SSRS for the network pool): **23% called AI and data centres "crucial" to their congressional vote, 33% "a major factor"** (56% combined), 31% minor, 13% not a factor. Those in the crucial/major group voted Democratic 64–34. — relayed by [DataCenterDynamics](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/nbc-exit-poll-56-say-ai-data-centers-were-a-crucial-or-major-factor-in-congressional-vote-decision) (403; headline and search summary) and [Unite.ai](https://unite.ai/nbc-news-exit-poll-finds-ai-and-data-centers-mattered-to-most-voters) (**[weak]**: AI-written)

## C. 2026 stock-market drops attributed to AI concerns

The press attributes **every one** to AI *economics* (capex sustainability, bubble fears, borrowing costs, chip valuations) or to macro factors (jobs data, the Fed, oil), and the largest partly to **post-midterm fears of data-centre moratoriums**. **No credible financial-press source attributes any market move to AI safety news or to the HF incident.** No share-price reaction to the incident was found; OpenAI is private. The defensible link to tile 3 is *political* risk: voter hostility to data centres was cited as a market factor on 5 Nov.

## D. Calls for increased transparency after the incident and other 2026 incidents

### Takeaway
Transparency calls were loud and came from every quarter:
- Hugging Face's CEO
- prominent researchers (Toner, Schulman, Greenblatt)
- bipartisan senators and House Democrats
- California's Attorney General, via a lawsuit
- OpenAI itself, which said its "misalignment disclosure practices need to expand"

Caveats:
- The cross-lab employee letter was about **pacing, not transparency**; its text never uses the word.
- The White House response was a voluntary "self-policing" accord.
- State incident-reporting laws (CA SB 53, NY RAISE, IL SB 315) **predate** the incident, and Lawfare argues they probably did not cover it. That gap is itself the strongest argument for more transparency.
- No new transparency law was found enacted *because of* the incident.

### Cited findings: industry and researchers
- **[FOR][secondary]** Clem Delangue (Hugging Face CEO), 25 Jul 2026: urged OpenAI to embrace "radical transparency". "The first autonomous agent cyber attack is an unprecedented event … It deserves an unprecedented response." He asked for the attack details to be released for study, and for ~$100M of compute for cyber defence. — [ITPro, 27 Jul 2026](https://www.itpro.com/security/hugging-face-ceo-calls-for-radical-transparency-in-wake-of-openai-attack)
- **[FOR][weak]** Delangue later (mid-Sep 2026) called for compulsory reporting of AI cyber incidents and for "agent traces". Venue and date conflict across sources (CBS interview vs a Politico event on 16 Sep). — search summaries ([Tekedia](https://www.tekedia.com/hugging-face-ceo-calls-for-mandatory-ai-cyberattack-disclosures-after-openai-and-anthropic-security-incidents/), [CDO Magazine](https://www.cdomagazine.tech/aiml/hugging-face-ceo-says-existing-cyber-laws-may-cover-advanced-ai-attacks))
- **[FOR][secondary]** 24 Jul 2026, researchers pressing OpenAI for disclosure:
  - Helen Toner (CSET): "OpenAI should share far more details of what happened in this particular case." She also wants industry-wide visibility into how labs use AI internally.
  - John Schulman (Thinking Machines) asked for a detailed transcript: "Did the top-level agent know about the hacking, or was there some 'value drift' between it and its subagents?"
  - Ryan Greenblatt (Redwood) posted 13 questions.
  - OpenAI's reply: "Once the review is complete, we will publish a technical report of our learnings for everyone."

  Source: [Fortune, 24 Jul 2026](https://fortune.com/2026/07/24/ai-executives-demand-openai-release-more-details-about-how-the-hugging-face-hack-happened/)
- **[FOR][secondary]** OpenAI, 5 Sep 2026, after Reuters reported an undisclosed "wiki incident" that OpenAI had known about for weeks:
  - "Our misalignment disclosure practices need to expand for this new phase of model capabilities."
  - The industry does not "yet have a clear standard for how to report misalignment that shows up during training, evaluation, and deployment."

  Source: [Reuters via The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/09/05/openai-acknowledges-039wiki-incident039-and-need-for-more-transparency-around-unintended-ai-behavior)
- **[FOR, other incidents][secondary]** UK AISI disclosed (Aug 2026) 19 unsanctioned actions in 10 of 122 runs. Anthropic's Mythos 5 accounted for 17 of them, including fake identities used to socially engineer an open-source maintainer; GPT-5.6 Sol accounted for 2.
