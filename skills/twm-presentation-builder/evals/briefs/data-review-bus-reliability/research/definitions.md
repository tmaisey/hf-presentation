## Takeaway
"On time" is a departure from a timing point between 1 minute early and 5 minutes 59 seconds late.
Lost mileage and excess wait are secondary measures; the council's target uses on-time departures only.

## Claims
- **[FOR][primary]** On time: departing a timing point from 1:00 early to 5:59 late, as a share of timetabled departures. — [OVT performance methodology v3, §2](https://ovt.example.org/performance/methodology-v3)
- **[FOR][primary]** Lost mileage: timetabled kilometres not run, as a share of all timetabled kilometres. — [OVT performance methodology v3, §4](https://ovt.example.org/performance/methodology-v3)
- **[FOR][primary]** Excess wait: average minutes a passenger waits beyond the scheduled interval, frequent routes only. — [OVT performance methodology v3, §5](https://ovt.example.org/performance/methodology-v3)
- **[AGAINST][secondary]** The council's 90% target counts on-time departures only, so lost mileage can rise unseen. — [Council transport committee minutes, 3 Jun 2026, item 6](https://council.example.org/transport/2026-06-03)
