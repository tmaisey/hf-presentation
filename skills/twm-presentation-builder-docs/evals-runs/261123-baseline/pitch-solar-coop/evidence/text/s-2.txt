## Reader notes
### Note 1 (for #s2-stat): Median of the bills residents shared
The audit covers the 48 flats on blocks C and D; 41 households shared their 2025 bills. The median annual spend was £612 per flat. Larkmoor Co-op energy audit 2025, p4
- source: Larkmoor Co-op energy audit 2025, p4 <https://larkmoor.example.org/energy-audit-2025>
### Note 2 (for #s2-title): Daylight hours
The audit counts spend between 08:00 and 18:00 as daylight: about 40% of the total. That is the share a rooftop array can supply directly. Larkmoor Co-op energy audit 2025, p6
- source: Larkmoor Co-op energy audit 2025, p6 <https://larkmoor.example.org/energy-audit-2025>
