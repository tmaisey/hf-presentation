## Reader notes
### Note 1 (for #s6-leaks): Residents’ main worry
Nine survey respondents named roof leaks during installation as their main worry. Blocks C and D resident survey, Sep 2026, Q5
- source: Blocks C and D resident survey, Sep 2026, Q5 <https://larkmoor.example.org/survey-2026-09>
### Note 2 (for #s6-tariffs): Single-sourced forecast
Single-sourced: one regional tariff forecast says prices may fall next year, which would shrink the saving. No second source supports or contradicts it. Regional energy outlook, Oct 2026
- source: Regional energy outlook, Oct 2026 <https://outlook.example.org/2026-10>
### Note 3 (for #s6-upkeep): Upkeep after the plan
One inspection and panel clean a year costs £1,150 for both roofs once the five-year service plan ends. Installer quotation Q-2291, §5
- source: Installer quotation Q-2291, §5 <https://installer.example.org/quotes/q-2291>
### Note 4 (for #s6-payback): Disputed payback
Disputed: the installer puts payback at about 7 years at current tariffs (relayed in the quote). The treasurer puts it at about 11 years once inverter replacement and a 2% yearly output loss are counted. The deck shows both. Installer quotation Q-2291, §6 Treasurer’s note to the board, 14 Oct 2026
- source: Installer quotation Q-2291, §6 <https://installer.example.org/quotes/q-2291>
- source: Treasurer’s note to the board, 14 Oct 2026 <https://larkmoor.example.org/board/2026-10-treasurer>
