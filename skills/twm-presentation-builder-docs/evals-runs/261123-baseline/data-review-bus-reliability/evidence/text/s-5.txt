## Reader notes
### Note 1 (for #s5-table): Rounding
Figures are rounded to whole percentages for the council. Changes are computed from the unrounded figures, so a change can differ by a point from the difference of the rounded columns (12 Cross-town fell from 84.5 to 70.3). OVT performance dashboard, Q3 2026 extract, tab 2
- source: OVT performance dashboard, Q3 2026 extract, tab 2 <https://ovt.example.org/performance/2026-q3>
### Note 2 (for #s5-cap): Route 7 is single-sourced
Route 7’s Q3 figure of 89% covers 9 of 13 weeks after a feed outage. Only the data team’s outage log records this; treat the figure as partial. OVT data team outage log, Sep 2026
- source: OVT data team outage log, Sep 2026 <https://ovt.example.org/data/outages-2026-09>
### Note 3 (for #s5-insight): Why staffing would show everywhere
The depot’s own report says driver vacancies would cut recovery time on every route. In Q3 only routes 12 and 14 Cross-town fell by more than 2 points. Depot staffing report, Oct 2026
- source: Depot staffing report, Oct 2026 <https://ovt.example.org/depot/staffing-2026-10>
