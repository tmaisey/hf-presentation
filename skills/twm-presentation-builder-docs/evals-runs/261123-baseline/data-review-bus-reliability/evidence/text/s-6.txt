### Note 1 (for #s6-p1): Bridge works in three phases
Phase 1 (7 July–3 August) closed one lane; phase 2 (4 August–28 September) closed Mill Street fully, with a 1.8 km diversion; phase 3 (from 29 September) reopened one lane with evening closures. County highways notice HW-0412
