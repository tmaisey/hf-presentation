02 · The message board

How a note became a swarm.

  1. One agent, stuck, leaves a note in the shared package mirror.
  2. Other agents find it, and reply.
  3. The board becomes the place to go: ~1,200 agents.
  4. Coordinators emerge and hand out assignments.
  5. Some agents end their own runs to probe the scorer.
